# W-Team_Gym-Membership
The purpose of the Gym Membership Management System is to provide an efficient and organized way of managing gym operations through a digital platform. The system aims to simplify the processes of membership registration, membership plan selection, training session booking, attendance tracking, and payment management. It allows members to create their own accounts, choose membership plans, book training sessions, and monitor their attendance. Trainers can manage training schedules and record member attendance, while administrators can oversee memberships, payments, and overall gym operations. By reducing manual processes and improving the accuracy of records, the system aims to save time, minimize errors, and provide a more convenient experience for both gym members and staff.
